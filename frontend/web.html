What we offer

Secure your payments with intelligent escrow

Buy and sell securely without fear of being scammed. ScrowPay holds your funds safely and uses AI to block fraudulent actors in real-time.

AI-Powered Verification

Our anomaly detection engine scores every transaction in real-time, flagging high-risk accounts before funds move. You're protected before you even know there's a threat.

Instant Settlements

Once both parties confirm, funds are released in seconds — not days. Powered by Squad API for instant payouts directly to any Nigerian bank account.

Dispute Protection

If something goes wrong, our mediation team steps in. We review evidence from both parties and ensure a fair resolution — always.

Real-Time Deal Chat

Every escrow comes with a built-in thread so buyer and seller communicate clearly, with a full paper trail attached to the transaction.
